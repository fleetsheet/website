## Introduction

Fleet uses cookies on the https://runfleet.com website to help ensure the best user experience for you. We take your privacy seriously, and we acknowledge that by using our website, you are giving us consent to the use of cookies and are providing information that should not be shared openly to people outside of our company. This document explains what cookies are, how we use them, why we use them and your choices regarding cookies.

## What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device or browser and allows our website to remember information about your visit, your preferences and other information. Cookies can be “persistent” or “session” cookies. Persistent cookies remain on your browsing device when you go offline, while session cookies are deleted as soon as you close your web browser.

## Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to distinguish you from other users of our website, in order to improve performance and ensure that we are providing the best user experience possible.

We use cookies mainly for collecting data for assessment of the website, for example how many visitors we receive to a page.

## How We Use Cookies

On our website, we use the following types of cookies:

- **Essential cookies:** These keep the website secure and working smoothly as you move between pages, for example by remembering the details you enter in a form. The website relies on them to run, so they are always active.
- **Preference cookies:** These remember your settings, such as your language and interface preferences, so the website looks and behaves the way you chose on your next visit.
- **Analytics cookies:** These help us understand how visitors use the website, such as which pages are visited most and how people find them. We use analytics tools such as Mixpanel for this, and we review the results in aggregate to improve the website.

## Your Choices Regarding Cookies

If you’d like to delete cookies or instruct your web browser to delete or refuse cookies, please visit the help pages of your web browser. Individuals under GDPR have certain rights to their information. You can learn more about this in the GDPR guide.

Please note that if you delete cookies or refuse to accept them, you may not be able to use all of the features we offer and you may not be able to store your preferences.
