## Introduction: The Hidden Costs of Manual Maintenance

Managing property maintenance with spreadsheets, paper logs, and phone calls might seem familiar — but it’s far from efficient. In today’s real estate environment, where teams juggle multiple sites, assets, vendors, and compliance demands, outdated systems come at a cost: lost time, reactive firefighting, and inconsistent reporting.

**If you’re still using spreadsheets to manage your building maintenance**, it might be time to switch.

---

## What Is Property Maintenance Software?

**Property maintenance software**, also known as a **CMMS (Computerized Maintenance Management System)/CAFM (Computer-Aided Facilities Management)**, helps real estate and facility operations teams automate, centralize, and track their maintenance workflows — all from one digital platform.

A good CMMS/CAFM allows you to:

- Log and assign maintenance tasks in real time
- Schedule preventive maintenance (PPM)
- Track asset history and costs
- Generate compliance and budget reports
- Access updates from any device (mobile-first)

## Why Spreadsheets No Longer Work

![](./images/real-estate-teams-smart-maintenance-software/spreadsheets.jpg)

Spreadsheets were never built to handle the scale and complexity of modern property operations. Real estate and facilities managers face challenges like:

- No automatic reminders for inspections or jobs
- No real-time status updates or technician tracking
- Difficulty managing vendors across multiple locations
- Zero audit logs for accountability or compliance

As your portfolio grows, **manual tracking creates bottlenecks and blind spots**.

## CMMS vs. Third-Party Property Managers: A Cost Comparison

Many real estate owners rely on third-party property managers, who typically charge **6–8% of total rental revenue** — and that doesn’t include service markups, staff time, or overhead. This can **double your OPEX (operational expenditure)** in some cases.

Switching to **self-management with a modern CMMS** like Fleet enables:

- Direct vendor and technician control
- Transparent cost logging
- Reduced middleman fees
- Faster workflows without markups

By eliminating layers of third-party management and digitizing your internal ops, companies can **save tens (or hundreds) of thousands annually**.
